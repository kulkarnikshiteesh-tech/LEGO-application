Why I'm the right fit now

01

Technology is a means, not the goal

Microcontrollers, sensors and code are tools I reach for to make a play idea real, never the point in themselves. I've worked across platforms like Arduino, Raspberry Pi and ESP32 and understand how they behave, so when an idea needs me to go deeper I do, comfortably. What I care about is the experience they unlock for the child.

02

I go as deep as the problem needs

Building Makeable on my own took me into application architecture, 3D computation, raytracing and manufacturing logic, developed alongside AI. It is proof that when a problem demands real technical depth, I get into it and deliver rather than hand it away.

03

More built, more owned

Since I last applied I have taken products end to end, from market research to cost optimisation to manufacturing, and grown a team of designers and engineers. A new range of STEM kits is now selling, with the No-code Robot in development. The output has grown, and so has the scope I can hold.

04

Why LEGO, specifically

The intersection of physical play, digital interaction and child development is exactly where my work already lives. I am not pivoting toward this role, it describes what I do. And there is no more aligned place on earth to do it than Billund. Not a stepping stone, a destination.

I've spent a year building more, learning more, and leading more. The work speaks for itself -- this page is the proof.
