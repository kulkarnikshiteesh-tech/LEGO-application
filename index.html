Why I'm the right fit now

01

Connected hardware, hands-on

Since my last application, Makerverse has an ESP32-based product actively in development. I've worked hands-on with Arduino, Micro:bit, and ESP32 -- designing, prototyping, and testing. This is the practical electronics prototyping this role requires, not delegated work.

02

More products, more depth

The products on this page didn't exist at my last application. [X] new products designed and shipped at Makerverse. Each one goes through the same child-centered, playful, manufacturable design process this role asks for -- and I've led every one of them.

03

This is already my day job

The intersection of physical play, digital interaction, and child development is exactly where Makerverse lives. I didn't pivot toward this role -- this role describes what I already do, at scale, with a real team, in real production. The evidence has grown.

04

Why LEGO, specifically

I'm applying because there isn't a more aligned place on earth for someone who designs play for children. Not as a stepping stone -- as a destination. Billund is where this kind of work is taken most seriously, and I want to be part of that.

I've spent a year building more, learning more, and leading more. The work speaks for itself -- this page is the proof.
